## English

### Data processed

The planned CLA workflow processes only data reasonably needed to verify and
audit contributor authorization:

- authenticated GitHub account identifier and public profile information;
- legal name and contact email supplied by the signer;
- confirmation that the signer has capacity and authority to grant the rights;
- repository, pull-request, agreement revision, and acceptance timestamp data;
- operational records needed to investigate signing-check failures or abuse.

The Project does not plan to request a home address, government identifier,
telephone number, employer name, or payment information through the individual
CLA form.

### Purposes and access

The data is used to present and record the agreement, determine whether a pull
request satisfies the CLA requirement, respond to correction or rights-chain
questions, prevent abuse, and preserve evidence that the Project may lawfully
use and license accepted Contributions.

GitHub usernames, pull requests, comments, and pass/fail check results may be
public. Custom signer fields and exported signature records will be restricted
to the Project Steward and specifically authorized compliance maintainers and
will not be committed to the public application repository.

### Service provider and location

The planned signing provider is the hosted
[CLA Assistant](https://cla-assistant.io/) service. Its published documentation
states that signature data is stored in a Microsoft Azure Cosmos DB deployment
in Europe. GitHub and CLA Assistant process data under their own terms and
privacy notices. Before activation, the Project will verify the current service
documentation and record the exact agreement Gist revision and repository
configuration.

### Retention and requests

Signature records and the agreement revision they prove will be retained for
as long as reasonably necessary to document the Project's contributor rights
chain, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Operational logs should be
kept only for the provider's or Project's documented security and reliability
needs. Private exports must be access-controlled and protected by the same
backup safeguards as other compliance records.

After activation, signers may request access to or correction of Project-held
signing data through `contact@okoklabs.com`. The address may be operated as a
forwarding alias with a monitored reply mailbox. A deletion request will be assessed
against applicable law and the Project's need to retain minimum evidence of
licenses already granted. Correcting or deleting contact data does not by
itself revoke rights already granted for Contributions.

## 中文

计划中的 CLA 流程只处理核验和审计贡献授权所必需的数据：GitHub 身份及公开资料、
签署人填写的法定姓名和联系邮箱、签署与授权资格确认、仓库和 PR、协议修订及签署
时间，以及排查签署故障或滥用所需的运行记录。

个人 CLA 表单目前不计划收集家庭住址、身份证件号码、电话号码、雇主名称或支付
信息。GitHub 用户名、PR、评论和检查是否通过可能公开；自定义表单字段与导出的
签署记录只允许项目负责人和明确授权的合规维护者访问，不提交到公共应用仓库。

计划使用托管版 [CLA Assistant](https://cla-assistant.io/)。该服务公开文档称签署
数据存储于欧洲的 Microsoft Azure Cosmos DB。GitHub 与 CLA Assistant 也会按
各自条款和隐私说明处理数据；正式启用前仍须核对其最新说明和实际配置。

签署记录及对应协议修订会在证明贡献权利链、处理争议和履行法律义务所合理需要的
期限内保留。启用后，签署人可通过 `contact@okoklabs.com` 请求查询或更正项目持有
的签署信息；该地址可以是转发别名，回复可从受监控的收件箱发出。删除请求需要结合适用法律和保留既有授权最低证据的必要性处理；更正
或删除联系信息本身不会撤回已经授予的贡献许可。
