# Support

## Community Support

- Reproducible product defects: use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/okoklabs/openlabstock/issues).
- Usage, deployment and design questions: use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/okoklabs/openlabstock/discussions) after Discussions is enabled.
- Security vulnerabilities: follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md); never open a public Issue.

Before asking for help, check the latest release, [`README.md`](./README.md), [`DEPLOYMENT.md`](./DEPLOYMENT.md), existing Issues and Discussions.

Provide the OpenLabStock version, deployment method, operating system, expected behavior, actual behavior and minimal logs with all credentials, addresses and personal data removed. Do not upload databases or backups.

Community support is provided on a best-effort basis without response-time or resolution guarantees. The issue tracker is not an emergency channel and cannot administer a privately operated server.

## Commercial Support

Commercial support and alternative licensing are not currently open for contracting. See [`COMMERCIAL.md`](./COMMERCIAL.md). A future support contract must state its own scope, hours, response targets, exclusions and data-handling terms; community participation does not create a service-level agreement.
